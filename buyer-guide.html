Financing Your Future Home

Protecting Your Loan Approval

Once pre-approved, your actions between approval and closing matter enormously. Follow these guidelines to protect your loan and ensure a smooth path to the closing table.

✓ Do This

  • Notify your lender of any address changes
  • Notify lender of salary/wage changes
  • Be ready to prove bank deposits
  • Acquire homeowner's insurance immediately after contract
  • Keep all debt paid and current

✗ Avoid This

  • Large purchases on existing credit
  • Applying for new lines of credit
  • Closing or transferring credit balances
  • Changing jobs without telling your lender
  • Co-signing for anyone else's loan

Have On Hand

  • ✓ Last 2 years' tax returns & W-2s
  • ✓ Last month of pay stubs
  • ✓ Last 3 months bank statements
  • ✓ Photo ID
  • ✓ 2 years of employer history
  • ✓ Real estate agreement copy

Glossary

Real Estate Terms Simplified

Appraisal
A written justification of the price paid for a property, based on analysis of comparable sales of similar homes nearby.
APR
Annual Percentage Rate — the yearly interest rate you'll pay on your mortgage, including lender fees. Usually slightly higher than your base rate.
Closing Costs
Generally 2–5% of the purchase price, covering lender fees, title insurance, recording fees, property taxes, and HOA fees.
Closing Disclosure
A document itemizing all funds paid or disbursed at closing. Review it carefully before your closing day.
Deed
The legal document that transfers title (ownership) of a property from seller to buyer.
Down Payment
A cash payment at closing — typically 3%, 5%, or 20% of the purchase price, depending on your loan program.
Earnest Money
A deposit placed in escrow after an offer is accepted, showing the seller you're entering the transaction in good faith.
Equity
Your financial interest in the property — the difference between the home's market value and what you still owe on the mortgage.
Escrow
Placing money or documents in the custody of a neutral third party until all conditions of a transaction are met.
HOA Fee
A monthly or annual fee for homes in communities with a Homeowners Association — covers maintenance of common areas and amenities.
Home Warranty
A contract covering repair or replacement of major home systems — HVAC, plumbing, electrical — for a set period after purchase.
Mortgage Insurance
Protects the lender if you default. Typically required when your down payment is less than 20% of the purchase price.
Pre-Approval
A lender's written commitment to loan you a specific amount, based on verified income, assets, and credit. Essential before making offers.
Property Taxes
Annual taxes imposed by local government on real property. In Palm Beach County, rates vary by city and are reassessed annually.
Title Insurance
Protects you from financial loss due to defects in the title discovered after purchase — liens, encumbrances, or ownership disputes.
